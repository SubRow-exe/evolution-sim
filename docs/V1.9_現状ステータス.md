# 5. Exp16 — fixed-iLUCA environment robustness

**55/55 runs完了。**

iLUCAを固定し、H2 concentration / exchange-loss timescale / diffusion / source geometryだけ変更。

主要結果:

```text
H2 1 mM  -> extinction
H2 3 mM  -> extinction
H2 6 mM  -> persistence / slow growth
H2 10 mM -> robust growth
H2 15 mM -> high growth

low diffusion  -> extinction
high diffusion -> high growth
fast H2 loss   -> extinction
slow H2 loss   -> very high growth

square layout  -> robust growth
cross layout   -> 10-day persistence / contraction
cluster layout -> local reproduction then extinction
```

中心的解釈:

> **iLUCA成立性はsource peak濃度だけでなく、H2-rich領域の面積・連結性・遭遇可能性に強く支配される。**

baseline referenceは当面維持:

```text
H2 source = 10 mM
D = 5e-9 m2/s
exchange/loss tau = 900 s
4-source square
```

Exp16結果を使ってbaselineを最適化し直さない。
